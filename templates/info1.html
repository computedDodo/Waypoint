How Waypoint works

1. Claim a mission

Sign in, enroll in a campaign you're invited to, and claim an open task. A countdown starts.

2. Execute & capture

Follow the mission instructions and take a clear screenshot proving what you found or completed.

3. Submit & earn

Upload before time runs out. Once approved, points land in your wallet and you can redeem them for cash or gift cards.

Privacy

We collect your email for authentication and notifications, and screenshots/notes solely to verify mission completion for the client running that campaign. Proof is retained only as long as needed to resolve any dispute, then removed.

Terms of use

Software you test through Waypoint belongs to the client running that campaign and is confidential. Don't share or publicly post screenshots of unreleased software without written consent. Accounts submitting false proof or breaching confidentiality may be suspended.
